The Collaborative for Intersectoral Modeling of the Earth System (CLIMES) is a collection of five research groups, including the HES lab. The goal of the Collaborative is to develop coupled modeling frameworks for understanding the impact of hazards on social, ecological, economic, and infrastructural systems. Early work has focused on wildfire and wildfire smoke in gateway communities and US National Parks.
