Lab head: Jason 'Jake' Hawes
======
Jake Hawes is an Assistant Professor in the School of Computing and School of Environment and Natural Resources at the University of Wyoming. Jake's interests lie in understanding and deploying mixed methods tools for modeling coupled natural-human systems. He explores these dynamics with mixed methods, interdisciplinary analysis. His recent and ongoing work includes: 
1. Exploring the relationship between natural hazards and community adaptive capacity
2. Understanding the role of urban agriculture in future cities via industrial ecology and spatial science
3. Improving models of infrastructure system resilience.  

The lab is recruiting a post-doc for these and other projects. Interested? Please send your CV and a brief statement of interests (no more than two pages) to Jake at jhawes@wyo.edu.
